\---

name: ui-qa

description: Use this skill when a frontend implementation is nearly complete and needs a final quality pass for UI, UX, responsiveness, and polish.

\---



Perform a final UI/UX review.



Check:

\- spacing consistency

\- visual hierarchy

\- responsive behavior

\- hover/focus states

\- semantic HTML

\- placeholder quality

\- animation consistency

\- broken layouts

\- contrast/readability

\- obvious low-quality areas



Return:

\- issues found

\- fixes applied

\- remaining risks
