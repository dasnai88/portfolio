description: Use this skill when adding or refining animations, scroll reveals, hover states, and motion behavior in a modern frontend UI.

\---



Use Framer Motion to improve polish without distracting the user.



Rules:

\- animation must support UX,

\- keep motion subtle and premium,

\- prefer fade, slide, stagger, and soft transitions,

\- avoid excessive motion,

\- preserve performance on mobile,

\- respect readability and accessibility.



Checklist:

\- hover states

\- section reveal

\- CTA motion

\- card motion

\- consistency across sections
